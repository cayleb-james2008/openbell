# OpenBell — Job (v0.8.0)

OpenBell is a local Live Kalshi Connected Trading and 15-minute institutional quant desk built in Rust: it connects directly to Kalshi v2 REST (Demo & Production) with RSA-PSS signing, live account/positions/orders/fills telemetry, fail-closed hard risk rails (disarmed by default with explicit arm confirmation and one-click Emergency Kill Switch), collects live public Kalshi 15m market tape across active crypto series (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE) with a toggleable background public collector (`Off / 60s / 180s` + adaptive 429 backoff + 200-window readiness gate progress), streams updates over SSE, scores 12 strategies on compound-sized paper slots with live sandbox backtesting, ranks strategies in an out-of-sample Paper Strategy Tournament with a 12×12 return correlation matrix, optimal uncorrelated 4-slot lineup (`|r| <= 0.65`), and entry-price × seconds-to-close Edge-by-Regime Heatmap, and computes PAV isotonic recalibration, EV-after-fees entry gating, and `$500/day` capital scaling diagnostics in Quant Lab.

A quant trader opens this desktop app to monitor live Kalshi balances, positions, resting orders, and fills under hard risk rails, stage and submit limit orders when explicitly armed (or trigger the Emergency Kill Switch in one click), track the live 15-minute tape with bid/ask spread history and background auto-collector cadence, inspect and live-backtest strategy entry rules, audit paper slot allocations with underwater drawdown and 3-strike rotations, and verify the OOS Strategy Tournament, 12×12 correlation matrix, Edge-by-Regime heatmap, PAV isotonic recalibration, 180-day scaling capacity & 5-gate readiness scorecard, 1,000-path Monte Carlo drawdown risk, and walk-forward IS/OOS stability.

1. What is the live Kalshi v2 account status (balance, portfolio value, positions, resting orders, fills), is live routing disarmed or armed, and are all hard risk rails passing?
2. Is the live Kalshi 15m auto-collector healthy and advancing toward the 200-window readiness gate, and what are the open 15-minute quotes, spreads, and settlement countdowns right now?
3. How is each strategy performing on paper, what are its entry rules, and how do edited rules backtest on collected tape?
4. How is simulated capital allocated across compound-sized slots, what are the underwater drawdown, stint attribution, 3-strike standings, and exportable daily audit report?
5. Which strategies lead the out-of-sample tournament, which uncorrelated 4-slot lineup (`|r| <= 0.65`) passes positive OOS expectancy, where does edge concentrate across entry-price × time-to-close regimes, and what do PAV recalibration, the `$500/day` scale planner, 5-gate readiness scorecard, Monte Carlo paths, and walk-forward splits say?
