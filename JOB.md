# OpenBell — Job (v0.7.0)

OpenBell is a local Kalshi 15-minute institutional quant research and paper compounding desk built in Rust: it collects live public Kalshi 15m market tape across active crypto series (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE) with a safe toggleable background public collector (`Off / 60s / 180s` + adaptive 429 backoff + 200-window readiness gate progress), streams updates over SSE, scores 12 strategies on compound-sized paper slots with live sandbox backtesting, ranks strategies in an out-of-sample Paper Strategy Tournament with a 12×12 return correlation matrix, optimal uncorrelated 4-slot lineup (`|r| <= 0.65`), and entry-price × seconds-to-close Edge-by-Regime Heatmap, computes PAV isotonic recalibration, EV-after-fees entry gating, and `$500/day` paper capital scaling diagnostics in Quant Lab, and publishes an honest daily audit trail — with zero order-placement capability anywhere in the binary.

A quant operator opens this screen to monitor the live 15-minute tape with bid/ask spread history and background auto-collector cadence, track the paper compounding pot and Stage 1 ($1,000) trajectory, inspect and live-backtest strategy entry rules, audit slot allocations with drawdown underwater and 3-strike rotations, and verify the OOS Strategy Tournament, 12×12 correlation matrix, Edge-by-Regime heatmap, calibration reliability (Wilson 95% CIs, Brier, ECE), PAV isotonic recalibration, 180-day paper scaling capacity & 5-gate readiness scorecard, 1,000-path Monte Carlo drawdown risk, and walk-forward IS/OOS stability.

1. What is the paper compounding pot, its curve, and its progress toward the $1,000 Stage 1 target?
2. Is the live Kalshi 15m auto-collector healthy and advancing toward the 200-window readiness gate, and what are the open 15-minute quotes, spreads, and settlement countdowns right now?
3. How is each strategy performing on paper, what are its entry rules, and how do edited rules backtest on collected tape?
4. How is paper capital allocated across compound-sized slots, what are the underwater drawdown, stint attribution, 3-strike standings, and exportable daily audit report?
5. Which strategies lead the out-of-sample tournament, which uncorrelated 4-slot lineup (`|r| <= 0.65`) passes positive OOS expectancy, where does edge concentrate across entry-price × time-to-close regimes, and what do PAV recalibration, the `$500/day` scale planner, 5-gate readiness scorecard, Monte Carlo paths, and walk-forward splits say?
